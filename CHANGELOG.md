## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-16

The first cut version, covering everything since the site went up. The history before this
point lives in the commit log; this entry summarises it.

- The ACT strata legislation reproduced in full: the Unit Titles Act 2001, the Unit Titles
  (Management) Act 2011 and its Regulation, and the Community Title Act 2001. Generated by
  `scripts/build.py` from the official `.docx` republications rather than from PDF text
  extraction, so the schedule tables and the heading hierarchy come through intact.
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and an IndexNow key, so search engines find the reader.
- A hub at the root, generated from the strata-kit manifest, with the legislation moved under
  `/legislation/`. Redirect stubs keep the documents' original root-level URLs working.
- The family navigation bar, rendered here and only here, and a card per project: lodger,
  former, noshow and stencil, with legislation listed last.
- noradz's mark as the favicon, and an "Open" button on each hub card in that card's colour.
