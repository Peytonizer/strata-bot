strata.

Reference material and small tools for strata work in the ACT. Everything here runs in your browser, and none of it is legal advice.

  • lodger

    Merge two PDFs and stamp every page.

    Merge two PDFs and stamp every page of the result — your seal or logo in the bottom-right corner, and a page number in a circle in the footer. Numbering runs unbroken across both documents and can start at any number.

  • former

    Fill and sign a flat PDF, or make one fillable.

    Fill and sign a flat PDF in a tab, or place the fields once and export the form still blank but now fillable, so everyone you send it to can type into it. For the scanned forms that arrive by email with no form fields in them.

  • legislation

    The ACT unit titles and community title Acts, in full.

    The full text of the four instruments that govern unit titles, community title and owners corporation management in the ACT, one plain page per document with the real heading hierarchy intact. Unofficial reproductions kept for reference and for feeding to an assistant — confirm current provisions at the ACT legislation register.

What this is

A side project, not a product.

The tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you open in them is uploaded anywhere, there is no account, and there is no analytics — each of them ships a content security policy that stops the page making a network request at all, which you can check in your browser's network tab.

None of it is legal advice, and the reproduced legislation is unofficial. For anything that matters, confirm the current provision at the ACT legislation register.

Built in the open — github.com/Peytonizer.
